## Feature status

- **Working:** create, rename, edit and delete documents. Rich text (headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, lists, quotes). Autosave and reopen. `.txt`/`.md`/`.docx` import. Owner, editor and viewer sharing with a My documents / Shared with me split. Server-side access control. Conflict detection and live pickup of collaborators' edits. Persistence in Postgres.
- **Not built:** real-time co-editing with cursors, comments, version history, export, sign-up, images and tables in the editor.
- **Next 2–4 hours:** version history with restore, Markdown/PDF export, Yjs real-time sync, login rate limiting.
